About Me

Geometry as motion. Graffiti as architecture.

3D

Layered forms, hard edges, and luminous vector rhythms inspired by cyber-futuristic street graphics.

I am PEWONSKI, an artist focused on geometric abstraction. My work centers on dynamic planes, overlapping polygons, and sharp vector-like lines that construct complex architectural rhythms on flat surfaces.

Operating across walls, physical canvas paintings, and digital paints, I explore the tension between high-contrast precision and raw street-level energy.
